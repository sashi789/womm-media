AI doesn't read words. It reads chunks. | What is a token, and why does it set AI's limits and price?

A token is a chunk of text. Before an AI reads anything, your text is cut into chunks.

How it works:
1. Your text is cut into chunks (the split shown is an example; real splits differ by model)
2. Each chunk has a number, an ID, from a fixed list
3. The model predicts the number of the most likely next chunk
4. That number becomes text, is added to the end, and it repeats

Rule of thumb for English: 100 tokens is about 75 words. Code and many other languages need more.
It also explains why AI can miscount the letters in a word: it sees chunks, not letters. And the context window and the price are both counted in tokens.
