Every report was a day off. Only at night. | How can a report be wrong only after dark?

A realistic scenario, not a claim about any company: a Chicago shop's report counts orders per day. Evening orders land on the next day.

Why it happens:
1. The database stores times in UTC, the world's standard clock
2. Chicago in winter is 6 hours behind UTC, so 8:30 pm Monday is 02:30 Tuesday in UTC
3. The report took the date straight from the stored time: Tuesday. The customer's day was Monday

The fix:
1. Keep storing UTC
2. Convert to the shop's time zone first, then take the date
3. Test orders just before and after midnight, in more than one time zone

Reference: Chicago uses Central Standard Time, UTC-6, in winter (IANA time zone database).
