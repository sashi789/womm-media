You tap Pay. Two banks agree in seconds. | How a UPI payment moves money

Your bank and the shop's bank never talk directly. NPCI sits in the middle and passes the messages.

Paying Rs 250 for chai, step by step (simplified):
1. You scan the QR and approve with your UPI PIN, which your bank checks
2. The request goes through NPCI to your bank, which checks your balance and takes Rs 250 out
3. NPCI asks the shop's bank to put Rs 250 in
4. Both banks send back a yes, and your app shows "payment successful"

The real cash moves later: banks settle with each other in batches, and only the net difference moves.

If money is cut but the payment fails, it should be reversed automatically. RBI's rule: by the next working day, or the bank owes you Rs 100 for each day it is late.
