"No blockers." What standup updates really mean.

"No blockers" means I'm stuck, but I'd rather not say. "Same as yesterday" means still stuck. "Almost done" means I haven't started the hard part. "I'll take a look" means I will forget this by lunch.

Which standup line do you use? Tell me in the comments.
