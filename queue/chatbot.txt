AI doesn't know. It guesses. | How a chatbot picks its next word

A chatbot is built on a language model. Its one job: given the text so far, predict what comes next.

How it works, step by step:
1. Text is cut into pieces called tokens
2. The model scores every possible next piece
3. It picks one, adds it to the text, and asks again, one piece at a time

It learned by reading enormous amounts of text: guess the next piece, check the real text, nudge its internal numbers, repeat. There is no table of facts inside, just patterns stored in numbers.

A setting called temperature controls how adventurous it is. Low: nearly always the top pick. High: sometimes lower picks, more creative and more mistakes. That is why asking twice can give two different answers.

And because it picks what sounds likely, not what it has checked, it can sound fluent and still be wrong. Check anything that matters.
