Launch day. One server. It melts. | How EC2 gets you more computers, fast

Amazon EC2 lets you rent virtual computers from AWS. Each one is a slice of a big physical server, walled off from the others. You click launch and it starts running in minutes.

Launching one means four choices:
1. Image: the starting software, like Amazon Linux
2. Size: how much CPU and memory (in t3.micro, t3 is the family and micro is the size)
3. Key pair: the key you log in with
4. Security group: who is allowed to connect

The part that surprises people:
- Running: billed per second, with a 60-second minimum
- Stopped: the instance charge stops, but the disk (EBS) is still billed
- Terminated: deleted for good
A forgotten instance keeps billing you.

On launch day you launch more instances to share the work, and Auto Scaling can add them when it is busy and remove them when it is quiet.
