Fine at 11:59. Dead at midnight. The expired certificate bug.

Nobody deployed anything, but at midnight every visitor got a full page security warning. The cause was an HTTPS certificate, which proves a site is really yours. Every certificate has an expiry date and this one ran out. The fix: renew automatically and alert weeks before any expiry date. Public certificates are now limited to 200 days, so this date comes round more often than ever.

(This is a realistic scenario, not a specific company's incident.)
