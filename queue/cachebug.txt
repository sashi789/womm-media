You logged in. As someone else. | How can a cache show one user another user's page?

A realistic scenario, not a claim about any company: a slow account page gets a cache, and the cache key is only the address, /account. Ana opens her page and it is saved. Ben opens his, and the cache hands him Ana's page: her name, her email, her orders.

Why it happens: the page depends on who is logged in, but the cache key doesn't say who is asking.

The fix:
1. Purge the cache and sign everyone out
2. Mark personal pages Cache-Control: private, no-store
3. Cache only pages that are identical for everyone, or put the user in the key
And test with two accounts, not one.
