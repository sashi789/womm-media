One typo. S3 down for hours. | How did a mistyped command take down S3 in US-EAST-1?

On February 28, 2017, AWS's own report says:
1. The S3 team was debugging a slow billing system. At 9:37 AM Pacific, a team member ran a playbook command to remove a few servers
2. One input was wrong, and a much larger set of servers was removed
3. They ran the index (where every object is) and placement (where new uploads go) subsystems. Both needed a full restart
4. Neither had been fully restarted in years in a region that large, so it took longer than expected
5. S3 was back to normal at 1:54 PM, about 4 hours 17 minutes later. EC2 launches, Lambda and the S3 console were affected too, and the status dashboard depended on S3

What changed: the tool now removes capacity more slowly with a minimum-capacity safeguard, services are split into smaller cells, and the dashboard spans regions.
