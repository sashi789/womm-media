Websites shouldn't know your password. | How do they check it without storing it?

They store a hash, a fingerprint of your password, not the password.

1. Sign up: the site hashes your password and saves only the fingerprint
2. Log in: it hashes what you typed and compares the two fingerprints
3. Salt: a random extra piece per user, so the same password gives a different hash for everyone
4. Slow on purpose: hashes like Argon2id and bcrypt make every guess expensive for a thief

Hashing is not encryption: a hash cannot be turned back. A good site never emails you your old password; it sends a reset link.
