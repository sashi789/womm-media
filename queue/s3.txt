What is Amazon S3? AWS storage explained in 60 seconds.

Imagine keeping every user photo on one server, and the disk dies. That is the problem S3 was built to solve.

In this episode:
- Buckets and objects: drop files in, it never fills up
- Why S3 almost never loses data: copies in three data centers, "11 nines" of durability
- Cold files move to cheaper storage automatically
- Private by default: you decide who gets in
